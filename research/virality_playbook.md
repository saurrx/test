# Virality playbook

Rules the make track must apply. `evidenced` = passed the pre-registered test with correction and leave-one-creator-out;
`prior` = craft belief not yet tested on views; `null` = tested, no effect found. Evidence is in `03_virality_analysis.md`.

| # | Rule | Status | Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| P1 | Full reels run 60–90 s; never under 30 s | evidenced (correlational) | F1: rho +0.11 detrended, q = 0.014; 32% of 60–90 s reels beat 2x baseline vs 12% under 30 s |
| P2 | Posting hour and weekday don't matter | null | F2: 1.03x and 0.95x, p > 0.7 |
| P3 | Space posts out; avoid bursts | evidenced (weak) | F4: 1.38x detrended, q = 0.02, 11/17 creators |
| P4 | Open with 0.3–1 s of picture, headline and sound before the first word; never more than 2 s | suggestive | F12: rho +0.27, p = 0.024; voice at 0.15–0.6 s 2.6x vs 1.0x at 0 s (small groups) |
| P5 | Captions: contrast ≥ 4.5, inside the safe area (y 260–1500 of 1920) | prior | QC gates |
| P6 | Put a headline of 3–5 words, large and high-contrast, on the very first frame | prior (craft) | Hook lab 1: the only variant with a frame-1 headline won 3/3 judges; the 4.8M-view reference also opens with one |
| P7 | Brightening the first frame alone does nothing; motion alone does little | prior (craft) | Hook lab 1: bright-only ranked last (3 pts), strike-at-0 mid (11) |
| P8 | Plan stories as 2–3 labelled parts; end each on a hook for the next; after a hit, post the follow-up next | evidenced (correlational) | F5: rho +0.17 between consecutive reels, p = 0.0004, 11/13 creators. F7: series-labelled reels 2.6x, p = 0.0016, 6/7 creators |
| P9 | Judge verdicts are craft opinions, not view predictions | calibration (2 runs) | Blind judges picked the real hit from the first 3 s in 59% of 118 judgments (first run 64–68%, second 53–56%; chance 50%), with a bias toward whichever clip is shown as "A" |
| P10 | Open on stakes with a concrete number and a contrast ("5,000 men failed. One prince."), not a bare fact, a question, or generic clickbait ("nobody told you this") | prior (craft) | Hook lab 2: both stakes variants ranked 1–2 of 8; generic clickbait 7th; plain number 8th; all 3 judges picked the stakes line as the strongest words |
| P11 | Put the headline over one strong focal image (glowing bow, sun), fully inside the frame from frame 1 | prior (craft) | Hook lab 2 judge fixes (3/3) |
| P12 | Hashtag count does not matter | null (interim, 324 reels) | F6: rho +0.06, p = 0.38 |
| P13 | Caption calls to action (comment / follow / share) are not a lever | null (interim) | F8: all p > 0.1, unstable across creators |
| P14 | Shiva / Mahadev topics may travel best; Krishna / Mahabharat worst, for these accounts | lead (exploratory) | F9: 2.3x and 0.44x, but only 3–5 creators each |
| P15 | Always voice-led, never music-only | evidenced | F10: 5/5 creators, p = 0.004; 94% of hits vs 71% of flops have a voiceover |
| P16 | A new picture at least every 6 s, ideally every 3–4 s; cut, dissolve or camera move all count | evidenced | F11: rho +0.25, q = 0.06, 11/16 creators; hits 1.6 vs flops 0.8 visual changes per 10 s. Hard cuts alone: null |
| P17 | Aim for likeability, not just a stop: hits earn about 1 like per 20 views vs a norm of 1 per 45. Give the viewer something worth saving or agreeing with (a usable rule, a satisfying pay-off) | evidenced (after-the-fact marker) | F17: rho +0.40, 15/17 creators; like rate 5.0–6.4% on hits vs 2.2–2.4% otherwise |
| P18 | Do not design endings around collecting comments | evidenced / null | F18: comment rate is lower on hits; F8: caption CTAs show no effect |
