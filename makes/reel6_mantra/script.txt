You chant this mantra. But do you know what you are saying? Om Namah Shivaya. Five syllables. Na. Ma. Shi. Va. Ya. It is called the Panchakshara: the five-letter mantra. And each letter is one element of your own body. Na is earth. Your bones. Ma is water. Your blood. Shi is fire. The heat in your belly. Va is air. Your breath. Ya is space. The silence inside you. So when you chant it, you are not calling a god far away. You are naming everything you are made of, and offering it to Shiva. So do not rush it. One syllable. One breath. Tomorrow morning, try it once. Slowly. Na. Ma. Shi. Va. Ya. Save this, so you remember the five. In part four: why Shiva wears the moon.
