*Five* things you must *never* offer to *Shiva.* Most people get at least one wrong. *One.* *Ketaki* flower. In the Shiva Purana, this flower *lied* for Brahma. Shiva banned it from his worship, forever. *Two.* *Tulsi.* Loved by Vishnu. But tradition keeps it away from the Shivling. *Three.* *Turmeric* and *kumkum.* They are for the goddess. Shiva is the ascetic. He takes *ash,* and sandalwood. *Four.* Water from a *conch.* Shiva destroyed the demon Shankhachuda. And the conch is said to come from his bones. *Five.* *Broken* rice. Every grain must be whole. A broken grain is an incomplete offering. So what does he *accept?* Plain *water.* A *bel* leaf, with three unbroken leaflets. And a *calm* mind. In part three: the one *mantra* to say while you pour. Follow. And *save* this, before your next temple visit.
