You've been ringing the temple *bell* *wrong.* Most of us hit it three, four times, and walk in. But the bell is *not* a doorbell for God. There is a *mantra* for it. *Aagamaartham* tu *devaanaam.* *Gamanaartham* tu *raakshasaam.* It means: let the *divine* come in. Let the *darkness* leave. So here is the *temple* *way.* *One.* Stop at the door. Empty your hands. *Two.* Ring it *once.* Just once, with your right hand. *Three.* Don't walk. A good bell rings for about *seven* seconds. Stand, till the sound is gone. Tradition says that sound cuts your thoughts for a moment. That *silence* is the real *darshan.* *Four.* Now step in, right foot first. The bell was never meant to wake God. It was meant to wake *you.* Tomorrow, ring it *once,* and count the seconds. How long did yours ring? *Comment* the *number.*
