Most people pour water on the *Shivling* *wrong.* They splash it, and leave. The *Shiva* *Purana* describes something else. A thin, *unbroken* stream. It is called *jal-dhaara.* Here is the *temple* *way.* *One.* Use a *copper* pot. Tradition avoids steel. *Two.* Never pour standing tall above him. *Sit,* or bend low. *Three.* Face *north.* *Four.* Pour slowly. One thin stream. Say *Om* *Namah* *Shivaaya,* until the pot is empty. Do not break the stream. And *five.* Never walk a full circle around the Shivling. Stop at the water channel, and *turn* *back.* Why? That channel carries what was offered to Shiva. You never step across it. In *part* *two:* what to offer on which day, and what you must *never* offer. Follow, so you don't miss it. How many of these five did you already know? *Comment* the number.
